Ask every customer for a Trustpilot review after their order. When an order is fulfilled, paid or placed, the app creates a Trustpilot email invitation for the customer, and Trustpilot sends it after the number of days you choose.

Connect your Trustpilot Business account with an API key and secret, choose when customers are asked, and set the sender, reply-to address, language and template of the email.

- **Invitations after every order.** Choose Order is fulfilled, Order is paid or Order is placed, and how many days Trustpilot waits before emailing the customer, from 0 to 90.
- **Your email, your template.** Set the sender name, reply-to address, language, invitation template, tags and the page customers see after their review.
- **Product reviews too.** Ask customers to review the products they bought as well, matched by SKU, with product names, images and links.
- **Status on every order.** Each order shows whether it was invited, when the email goes out and any error, and the orders list shows the status in a column.
- **One invitation per order.** Orders already invited are skipped, and when Trustpilot doesn't confirm an invitation, the app marks the order instead of risking a second email. Send an invitation again from the order.
- **Past and selected orders.** Invite recent orders placed before you installed the app, or select orders in the list and invite them in one go. Runs go in the background and show on the app's Workflows tab.

Setup takes a few minutes. You need a Trustpilot plan with the API module, which Enterprise plans include. In Trustpilot Business, create an API application under Integrations > Developers > APIs and copy its API key and secret. Paste them in the app settings, save, and choose Actions > Test connection to find your business unit ID and list your invitation templates.
